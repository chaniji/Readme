# ADHD - Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder

## Definition
Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) is a neurodevelopmental disorder characterized by persistent patterns of inattention and/or hyperactivity-impulsivity that interfere with functioning or development.

## Core Characteristics

### Inattention
- Difficulty sustaining attention in tasks or play
- Appearing not to listen when spoken to
- Difficulty organizing tasks and activities
- Losing things necessary for tasks (keys, wallet, phone)
- Easily distracted by extraneous stimuli
- Forgetfulness in daily activities

### Hyperactivity-Impulsivity
- Fidgeting or squirming
- Inability to remain seated when expected
- Feeling restless
- Difficulty engaging in activities quietly
- Talking excessively
- Difficulty waiting turns
- Interrupting or intruding on others

## Impact on Daily Life
- **Work/Studies:** Difficulty with task completion, time management, organization
- **Relationships:** Challenges with communication, listening, impulse control
- **Mental Health:** Higher rates of anxiety, depression, low self-esteem
- **Productivity:** Time blindness, procrastination, task initiation difficulties

## Management Strategies
- **Structured Environment:** Clear routines, organized workspace
- **Time Management Tools:** Timers, reminders, task lists, calendar systems
- **Breaks & Movement:** Regular breaks to manage restlessness
- **Medication:** When clinically recommended
- **Therapy:** Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), coaching
- **Digital Tools:** Apps for focus, task management, time blocking

## Working Effectively with ADHD
- Break tasks into smaller steps
- Use external reminders and timers
- Create distraction-free work zones
- Prioritize high-focus work during peak energy times
- Build in buffer time for tasks
- Regular reviews and adjustments to systems

## Resources
- Consult healthcare professionals for proper diagnosis and treatment
- ADHD coaching for personalized strategies
- Support communities and forums
