# Obsidian Web Clipper

Highlight and capture web pages in your favorite browser. Save anything and everything with just one click.

## Features
- **Capture**: Metadata and pages to durable files.
- **Highlight**: Passages are always visible.
- **Templates**: Custom rules for different sites.

## Privacy and Freedom
- **Local Storage**: Your thoughts are yours.
- **No Lock-in**: Markdown and JSON exports.
